# Example: angle-finder

**Fictional.** Northwind Rentals and its market are invented.

## Input

> Topic: maintenance updates.

Loaded: `foundation/positioning.md`, `foundation/competitors.md`.

## Output

Consensus take: residents want faster maintenance, so operators need faster techs.

| # | Move | Hook | Ties to | For | Distance | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Invert | Your techs are fast enough. Your updates aren't. | Value | VP of Operations | 3 | 3 |
| 2 | Zoom in | One leak, one tech, and the office phone that wouldn't stop ringing. | Attributes | Regional Property Manager | 3 | 3 |
| 3 | Cost | Every "is anyone coming" call is a leasing tour that didn't happen. | Value | VP of Operations | 2 | 3 |
| 4 | Who's missing | The maintenance tech is the one residents blame when nobody tells them anything. | Value | Resident Experience Lead | 3 | 2 |
| 5 | Before and after | The same Tuesday work order, with and without a text when it's scheduled. | Attributes | Regional Property Manager | 2 | 3 |
| 6 | Admit the limit | Northwind won't make your techs faster. It'll make the wait quieter. | Category | VP of Operations | 3 | 2 |

Recommended: #2 because a single moment is easy to picture and leads straight to the attribute without a pitch.
