## Output

Account: founder
Idea: maintenance calls come from missing updates, not slow techs.

```
A leak in Building C. One tech on it within the hour.

The office phone still rang all morning.

Every call asked the same thing. Is anyone coming? The tech was already there. Nobody had told the residents.

That's the part we kept seeing in leasing offices. The work gets done, but the update never leaves the work order.

So the update goes out when the work order changes. Scheduled, then done. Residents stop calling to ask.

What does your team do when the fix is fast but the phone still rings?
```

Alternate hooks:
1. The tech fixed the leak in an hour. The phone rang until noon.
2. Residents don't call because maintenance is slow. They call because it's quiet.
