# Example: positioning

**Fictional.** Northwind Rentals, its features and its competitors are invented.

## Input

> We shipped Spanish-language notices last week. Does the positioning need to change?

Loaded: `foundation/company.md`, `foundation/competitors.md`, `foundation/personas.md`.

## Output

### 1. Competitive alternatives

No change. Personal phones, the PMS messaging module, paper notices and dedicated vendors. **[Fact]** competitors.md

### 2. Unique attributes

Add: notices sent in the resident's preferred language, chosen per unit. **[Unknown]** Shipped per the request, but company.md doesn't list it yet. Confirm before it goes public.

### 3. Value

Onsite teams stop printing two versions of every notice. **[Inference]** Follows from the paper-notice alternative, not from customer feedback.

### 4. Who cares most

No change to the segment. Regional Property Managers in markets with many Spanish-speaking residents care most. **[Inference]**

### 5. Market category

No change. Resident communication software for apartment operators. **[Fact]** positioning.md

### 6. Relevant trends

No change.

### Short version

No change. Language support is an attribute, not a new story.

### Changes from current

- Step 2 gains one attribute, pending confirmation in company.md.
- Step 3 gains one value line.
- Nothing else moves. Rewriting the whole doc for one feature would make the story harder to repeat.

Saved to `outputs/positioning-draft-2026-11-01.md`. foundation/positioning.md is untouched until a person approves.
