# Voice

**Load this file when:** drafting or reviewing anything a person outside Northwind will read.

**Fictional.** This voice card belongs to Northwind Rentals, an invented company. Replace it with your own.

## Who's talking

Northwind sounds like a former leasing office manager who now builds software. Practical, a little dry, never salesy. Talks about residents and onsite teams as people, not "users."

## Rules

- Lead with what changes for the onsite team, then explain how.
- One idea per paragraph. Paragraphs of one to three sentences.
- Use contractions. "It's" over "it is."
- Name the specific moment: a water shutoff notice, a 7 a.m. call about a leak. Skip abstractions.
- Numbers need a source from metrics.md or company.md. No source means TBD, or cut the number.
- End posts with a question or an observation, not a call to action.
- Write at roughly an eighth-grade reading level.

## Punctuation

- No em dashes or en dashes. Use a period or a comma.
- No semicolons.
- No exclamation marks.

## Sounds like Northwind

> The water's off in Building C from 9 to noon. Residents who got the text didn't call. The two who only got the paper notice did.

> Most leasing offices don't need another tool. They need the tools they have to stop sending the same message three times.

## Doesn't sound like Northwind

> Revolutionize your resident experience with our cutting-edge, all-in-one communication platform.

> We're thrilled to announce an exciting new feature that will transform how you engage residents.
