# Company

**Load this file when:** a skill needs to say what Northwind sells, who buys it, or why it's different.

**Fictional.** Northwind Rentals is an invented company used to show how the repo works.

## One line

Northwind Rentals gives apartment operators one place to message residents, so notices get read and maintenance updates stop living in five inboxes.

## What it sells

- **Resident messaging.** Text, email and in-app notices sent from one inbox, with read receipts per unit.
- **Maintenance updates.** Residents see when a work order is scheduled, started and closed without calling the office.
- **Building announcements.** Water shutoffs, package room changes and amenity closures go to the right buildings, not the whole portfolio.

## Who buys it

Mid-size apartment operators, roughly 2,000 to 20,000 units, running their own property management. The full rubric lives in icp.md.

## Why it exists

Onsite teams send the same notice by text, email, paper and a sticky note on the elevator, then still field calls from residents who missed it. Northwind's founder ran leasing offices before starting the company and built the first version for her own team.
