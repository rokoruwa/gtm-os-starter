# Competitors

**Load this file when:** positioning, picking angles, or answering "how is this different."

**Fictional.** Every vendor named here is invented. None is a real company.

## Alternatives a buyer weighs

| Alternative | What it is | Where it wins | Where it falls short |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal phones | Onsite staff text residents from their own numbers | Free, fast, residents reply | No record, leaves when the employee leaves |
| PMS messaging module | Messaging built into the property management system | Already paid for, tied to the resident ledger | Email-first, few read receipts, hard to target by building |
| Paper notices | Printed notices on doors and in elevators | Everyone sees the elevator | No proof of delivery, costs onsite hours |
| TenantPing (fictional) | Dedicated resident texting vendor | Strong SMS delivery | Text only, no maintenance status |
| Hallway Notes (fictional) | Resident portal with an announcements board | Nice resident app | Residents have to open the app to see anything |

## How to talk about them

- Compare against the alternative, not the vendor. "Most teams text from personal phones" works. "TenantPing is bad at email" doesn't.
- The real competitor is usually the status quo. Most accounts in icp-list.csv still use email or personal phones.
