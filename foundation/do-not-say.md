# Do not say

**Load this file when:** drafting or reviewing anything external. The pre-publish gate checks every draft against this file.

**Fictional.** This list belongs to Northwind Rentals, an invented company. Replace it with your own.

The test suite reads the "Banned words" section below, so keep one entry per bullet.

## Banned words

- revolutionize
- cutting-edge
- game-changer
- seamless
- leverage
- synergy
- delve
- all-in-one
- best-in-class
- world-class
- thrilled
- excited to announce
- transform
- empower
- unlock

## Banned punctuation

- Em dash and en dash
- Semicolon
- Exclamation mark

## Banned claims

- Any customer count, unit count, revenue figure or percentage that isn't sourced in metrics.md or company.md.
- Naming a customer, prospect or partner without written permission on file.
- "Guaranteed" anything, including renewal rates, response times or savings.
- Comparisons that name a competitor's flaws. Compare against the alternative, not the vendor.
- Legal or fair housing claims. Route those to counsel.

## Banned formats

- Hashtag walls. Two hashtags at most.
- Emoji at the start of lines.
- "Comment YES for the link" engagement bait.
