# Ideal customer profile

**Load this file when:** scoring an account for fit, choosing who to write to, or deciding whether a lead is worth a follow-up.

**Fictional.** This rubric describes Northwind Rentals' invented market.

## Fit rubric

Score each criterion 0, 1 or 2. Add them up for a score out of 10.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Portfolio size | Under 1,000 units | 1,000 to 2,000 or over 20,000 units | 2,000 to 20,000 units |
| Management model | Fee manager for third parties only | Mixed | Owner-operator, self-managed |
| Asset type | Single-family or student only | Mixed | Conventional multifamily |
| Resident messaging today | Already on a dedicated messaging tool, under contract | Built into the property management system, rarely used | Email, text from personal phones, paper notices |
| Signal | None found | One public signal | Two or more public signals |

## Signals that count

- A job post for a resident experience or resident retention role.
- Public resident reviews that mention missed notices or slow maintenance updates.
- A recent portfolio acquisition, which means new buildings to onboard.
- A new head of operations in the last six months.

## Disqualifiers

Any one of these sets the score to 0, whatever the rubric says.

- Fewer than 500 units.
- Signed a multi-year contract with a resident messaging vendor in the last year.
- Outside the United States. Northwind's carrier setup only covers U.S. numbers.

## Score bands

| Score | Meaning | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| 8 to 10 | Strong fit | Founder email this week |
| 5 to 7 | Possible fit | Add to nurture, revisit next quarter |
| 0 to 4 | Poor fit | Don't contact |
