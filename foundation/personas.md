# Personas

**Load this file when:** choosing who a piece is for, scoring fit, or picking which pain to lead with.

**Fictional.** These are invented buyer types for Northwind Rentals, not real people.

## VP of Operations

- **Owns:** resident retention, onsite staffing, the operating budget.
- **Pain:** renewals slip when residents feel ignored, and onsite teams burn hours repeating the same notice.
- **Wants to hear:** fewer calls to the leasing office, renewals that hold, one tool instead of four.
- **Doesn't want:** another login for onsite staff, a rollout that takes a quarter.
- **Proof they trust:** a peer operator's story, a two-week pilot at one property.

## Regional Property Manager

- **Owns:** five to fifteen properties and the managers who run them.
- **Pain:** can't see which buildings got a notice and which didn't until residents complain.
- **Wants to hear:** read receipts by building, templates the whole region uses.
- **Doesn't want:** to train every onsite manager from scratch.
- **Proof they trust:** a walkthrough with their own buildings loaded in.

## Resident Experience Lead

- **Owns:** resident satisfaction surveys, reviews, events.
- **Pain:** public reviews keep calling out missed notices and silent maintenance requests.
- **Wants to hear:** residents know what's happening without calling.
- **Doesn't want:** messages that feel like marketing.
- **Proof they trust:** before and after review themes from a similar portfolio.

## Who signs

The VP of Operations signs. The Regional Property Manager decides whether it gets used. Write to the person who feels the pain, and give them something to forward to the person who signs.
