# Positioning

**Load this file when:** writing anything public, picking an angle, or checking whether a claim fits the story.

**Fictional.** This is Northwind Rentals' invented positioning, written with the positioning skill.

## 1. Competitive alternatives

What a buyer does if Northwind didn't exist: texts from onsite staff's personal phones, the messaging module inside their property management system, paper notices on doors, or a dedicated resident messaging vendor. See competitors.md.

## 2. Unique attributes

- One inbox for text, email and in-app messages, with read receipts per unit.
- Maintenance status updates sent automatically from the work order.
- Notices targeted by building, floor or unit list in under a minute.

## 3. Value those attributes create

- Onsite teams stop sending the same notice three ways.
- Regional managers can see who got a notice before residents complain.
- Residents stop calling the office to ask whether maintenance is coming.

## 4. Who cares most

Self-managed operators with 2,000 to 20,000 conventional units, where the VP of Operations owns retention and onsite headcount. See icp.md.

## 5. Market category

Resident communication software for apartment operators. Not a CRM, not a property management system, not a marketing tool.

## 6. Relevant trends

- Residents expect the same message tracking they get from delivery apps.
- Operators are buying more buildings with the same onsite headcount.

## The short version

Northwind gives apartment operators one place to message residents, so notices get read and onsite teams stop repeating themselves.
